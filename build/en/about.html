About AIHCN

We build tools we'd pay for ourselves.

AIHCN is a small, independent studio. No investors, no growth-at-all-costs, no vaporware. Just practical AI tools for people who make content.

The Story

A domain experiment, rebuilt around one question

aihcn.com started as a content experiment — a machine-generated news site. It had traffic mechanics but no real users and no reason to exist. We threw it out and asked a better question: what would we actually pay for?

The answer was small, boring, useful tools: transcribe a video and search it, clip a long video into shorts, write metadata that isn't generic. Tools that save creators hours for less than the price of a coffee.

So that's what AIHCN is now: a studio that builds one useful tool at a time, tests it honestly, and ships it when it's actually good.

The Name

AIHCN = AI Hub for Creators

AI is what we build with. H is the hub — the middle ground between overwhelming platforms and disconnected one-off scripts. C is for creators, the people we build for. N is the network of tools and guides around it.

Four letters, one promise: AI that works for you, not the other way around.

Values

What we believe

Honesty

Status and results, both transparent

If something is in development, it says in development. If a tool doesn't deliver, we say so and fix it.

Measure

Value before features

Every tool must save more time than it costs. That's the whole product philosophy.

Long-term

Compounding, not hype

Content builds audience, audience builds revenue, revenue builds better tools. We don't chase trends.

Let's Talk

Ideas, feedback, or just saying hi

Tell us what slows you down as a creator. That's literally how we decide what to build next.
