Privacy Policy

Your data is yours. It is not our product.

Effective: 2026-08-04 (fully in force when tools launch)

1. What we collect

Only what's needed to provide the service:

  • Contact information: name, email, and message from the contact form; email from newsletter/waitlist signups.
  • Product data: after launch, the videos, links, and settings you upload to generate results.
  • Payment information: we never store your card or payment details. Payments are handled by third parties (Alipay, WeChat Pay, Paddle, etc.).

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and feedback;
  • To send subscription updates, product news, and necessary notices (unsubscribe anytime);
  • To provide and improve the tools (analytics uses anonymous, aggregated data only).

3. What we will never do

  • No selling: we will never sell or rent your personal information.
  • No training: your uploads are never used as training data for AI models.
  • No sneaking: we won't contact you unsolicited or grant third parties access to your data without your request.

4. Third-party services

The following third parties may process your data to provide the service, each bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare (hosting and protection);
  • Alipay, WeChat Pay, Paddle / Lemon Squeezy (payments — we never see your full payment details);
  • Email provider (sending subscriptions and notices).

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on Cloudflare's global edge network and related services. Account data is retained for 30 days after you delete your account or cancel your subscription, then removed. Newsletter emails are removed 30 days after you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

You may: request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, request deletion, or withdraw consent. Email hello@aihcn.com and we'll act within 30 days.

7. Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 16. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 16, we'll delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced by email or prominently on the site. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@aihcn.com
